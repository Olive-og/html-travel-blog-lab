"Adventures in Fire Island"

My trip to Fire Island was a relaxing and beautiful experience. Since cars aren't allowed on most of the island, I got around by walking on wooden boardwalks and riding a ferry, which made everything feel calm and peaceful. I spent time relaxing on the sandy beaches, exploring the Sunken Forest with its twisty old trees, and watching for deer that wandered close to the trails. The mix of ocean views, quiet nature trails, and charming small towns made Fire Island a really special place to visit.
